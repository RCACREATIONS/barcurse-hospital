When should you see a doctor?

Knowing when to ask for help can make care simpler and recovery smoother.

It is easy to put off a health concern when life is busy. But a timely conversation with a clinician can give you clarity, reassurance or an earlier path to treatment.

Pay attention to changes

A symptom that is new, persistent, getting worse or stopping you from doing the things you normally do is worth discussing. The same is true when you feel that something is not quite right, even if you cannot name it yet.

Do not wait for the perfect moment

You do not need to arrive with a diagnosis. Bring what you have noticed, how long it has been happening and any questions you have. Your care team can help you work out what comes next.

  • Book a visit for symptoms that persist or return.
  • Bring a list of medicines and relevant medical history.
  • Seek urgent help for severe pain, breathing difficulty, sudden weakness or loss of consciousness.
